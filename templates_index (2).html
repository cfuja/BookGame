How It Works

Create your book in a few simple steps:

  1. Add Characters: Create at least 2 characters with details like name, age, and motivations.
  2. Set Book Details: Choose genre, setting, tone, themes, and more (most fields required).
  3. Generate Plot: Build a story outline based on your inputs.
  4. Preview Structure: See a summary of your book’s chapters (optional).
  5. Generate Book: Create a full book (100–400 pages, 10–100 chapters), view it on-screen, and download as Markdown.
